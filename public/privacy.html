Privacy Policy

AP CSA Adaptive Practice Tool · Last updated August 2026

This tool is a self-paced practice application built and operated by Mr. Morgan for use by his own AP Computer Science A and Computer Science 3 students. It is not a commercial product and is not operated by a third party or vendor.

What information is collected

  • A student's first name and last initial only (for example, "Jane D."), not their full legal name
  • The last two digits of a student's school ID number, used only to tell apart students with matching names, and visible only to the teacher, never to students
  • A 4-digit PIN used to access the student's own account within the tool
  • Practice activity: which questions were answered, whether answers were correct, and current progress through course topics

What is NOT collected

  • Full legal names, addresses, birthdates, or other personally identifying information
  • Full school ID numbers
  • Any data linked to outside advertising, analytics, or tracking services
  • Any health, biometric, or location information

How information is stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service operated by Google. Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than running this practice tool for Mr. Morgan's own students.

Who can access this information

  • A student can only view their own progress after entering their own 4-digit PIN
  • The teacher dashboard is protected by a separate password known only to Mr. Morgan
  • No other individuals or organizations are given access to this data

Data retention and deletion

Mr. Morgan can export or permanently delete any or all student data directly within the tool at any time, including at the end of a school year or upon request.

Contact

Questions about this tool or its data practices can be directed to Mr. Morgan directly through his school email.
